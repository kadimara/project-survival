Standalone tuner for project-survival's wasteland/boulder-structure generator. Mirrors mulberry32/makeSimplex2D/fbm from src/worldgen/worldgen.ts exactly, plus the radius-3 spawn-safety carve from buildStones. Walkable ground is the majority "wasteland"; solid stone forms the rare separated "boulder structures" the game embeds scavengeable resources in (see buildWorldTiles in src/state/state.ts). The preview uses land/rock colors for clarity — the real game still renders stone/dirt tile colors, this is just about shape/coverage. Nothing here touches the app; copy the output block into src/constants.ts / src/worldgen/worldgen.ts when you're happy with a config.
